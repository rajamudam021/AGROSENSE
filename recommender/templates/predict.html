Parameter Guide
Nitrogen (N) Promotes leaf & shoot growth. Optimal: 40-80 kg/ha for most crops. Range: 0-200.
Phosphorus (P) Critical for root development & flowering. Optimal: 25-50 kg/ha. Range: 0-200.
Potassium (K) Improves drought tolerance & fruit quality. Optimal: 25-50 kg/ha. Range: 0-200.
pH Level Ideal: 6.0-7.0 for most crops. Below 5.5 = acidic, above 7.5 = alkaline. Range: 0-14.
Temperature Average growing season temperature. Most crops: 15-35°C. Range: -10 to 55°C.
