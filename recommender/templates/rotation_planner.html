How Crop Rotation Works
Legume Effect

Legumes (soybean, chickpea, lentil) fix atmospheric nitrogen into the soil, reducing fertilizer needs for the next cereal crop.

Pest Break

Different crops have different pest and disease cycles. Rotation prevents build-up of soil-borne pathogens specific to any one crop.

Nutrient Balance

Each crop removes different ratios of N, P, K. Rotating crops with complementary nutrient demands maintains soil fertility.
